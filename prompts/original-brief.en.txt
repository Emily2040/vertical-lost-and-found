Build THE VERTICAL LOST & FOUND: a climbing puzzle across a monumental wall of drawers containing the belongings of an absent city. The wall fills a cavernous railway hall. Begin with a drawer bursting open beneath the player; its rolling contents tip a receiving tray, which hauls another drawer outward to catch their next jump.

PROJECT-SPECIFIC BRIEF

The player climbs the storage system while rearranging its loads. Pull drawers along constrained rails, lock them at visible notches, open a cargo hatch and transfer a few substantial objects between connected trays. Exposed cables and counterweights explain every coupled motion. The same drawer can serve as a step, a moving lift or a counterweight depending on its position and load.

Use full-scale architecture and identifiable possessions: a trunk, a rolled carpet, a ceramic parcel, an umbrella case and boxed instruments. Build the drawers, rollers, latches, load cages and transfer chutes in Blender. Generate Nano Banana textures for handled lacquer, labelled luggage canvas and worn interior lining. Add exact drawer numbers and weight markings with deterministic text. Avoid visual clutter that conceals footholds.

Use a bounded number of rigid payloads, rail constraints and coupled counterweight equations. Character weight must affect an unlocked loaded tray. Reject latching where no notch exists; reject an impossible connection visibly. Keep the character controller's moving-platform velocity consistent with the mechanism. Preserve payload identity and mass during transfers.

Stage one creates a staircase with three drawers and one movable case. Stage two transfers that load between linked drawers to raise a lift while preserving a return ledge. Stage three ascends a vertical route using a limited set of objects, dispatching loads through chutes so platforms arrive where and when the player needs them. Save a recoverable checkpoint before each commitment; failure must explain the missing support or overloaded mechanism.

Test balanced and unbalanced loads, latch release, cargo jams, riding platforms and reset while objects move. Deliver a one-minute 1080p60 recording beginning with the drawer catch, then showing a visible load transfer and the hardest ascent. Finish by opening the final drawer from inside: daylight reveals a rooftop garden beyond the enormous storage wall. The route and ending must be earned through the working system.
